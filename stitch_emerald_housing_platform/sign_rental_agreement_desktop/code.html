Lease Terms & Conditions

1. Occupancy and Use

The premises shall be used only as a residential dwelling for no more than four (4) persons. Any guest staying longer than fourteen (14) consecutive days shall require prior written consent from Emerald Housing Management.

2. Rent and Payment Schedule

Tenant agrees to pay $4,200.00 per month, due on the first day of each calendar month. A late fee of $150.00 will be applied if payment is received after the 5th day of the month.

3. Maintenance and Care

Tenant shall maintain the property in a clean and sanitary condition. The Azure Heights complex provides weekly maintenance for balcony greenery, but interior cleanliness remains the responsibility of the tenant.

4. Security Deposit

A security deposit of $8,400.00 (two months' rent) is required prior to move-in. This deposit will be held in a non-interest-bearing escrow account and returned within 30 days of lease termination, minus any documented damages.

5. Pet Policy

Small pets (under 25lbs) are permitted with a non-refundable pet deposit of $500.00. No more than two pets allowed.
